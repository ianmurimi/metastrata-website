Jua Kenya privacy policy

Jua Kenya is made by Metastrata AI, Kenya. Effective 1 November 2026.

The short version

  • You play without an account: no email, phone number or password.
  • We keep only what the game needs: the nickname and county you choose, your Daily 5 results, and an anonymous player number.
  • Your nickname is never shown to other players. County Wars only shows counties and totals.
  • No ad tracking, no location, no contacts. We never sell or share your information. Sponsors only ever see totals.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see “Deleting your data”).

Who we are

Jua Kenya is a daily quiz app about Kenya, made by Metastrata AI in Kenya (“we”, “us”). We decide how the information described here is used. Contact us at support@juakenya.co.ke.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, or any advertising ID, and the app contains no advertising or tracking tools from other companies.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, your phone schedules them itself at the times shown in the app. We don’t send push messages from a server and don’t collect a notification address. Notifications never contain adverts. You can switch them off in the app (Me → Reminders) or in your phone’s settings.

Sponsors

Some partners sponsor parts of the app, such as a slideshow on the Today screen. Tapping a sponsor’s “Learn more” opens their website or WhatsApp in your browser, which is covered by their own privacy policy. Sponsors receive only totals (for example, how many phones saw a banner each day); never anything about you.

Companies that help us run the app

  • Supabase hosts our database and the anonymous sign-in.
  • Wikimedia Commons supplies the photos; your phone loads them directly from Wikimedia’s servers, which receive your device’s internet address like any website.
  • Google Play (and, later, Apple’s App Store) delivers the app and will handle any future subscription payments; we never see your card or M-Pesa details.

They process information only to provide their service to us. Some of them store data outside Kenya; we choose providers that protect it with appropriate safeguards, as the Data Protection Act allows.

How long we keep it

Your game results stay while your anonymous player number is in use, so County Wars totals remain correct. Reports and suggestions are kept for as long as they help us improve the questions. Anonymous counters are totals and contain nothing about you. If you uninstall the app, everything stored on your phone is deleted; ask us (below) to delete what’s in our database too.

Deleting your data, and your other rights

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Because you play anonymously, we find your information by your support code:

  1. In the app, open Me and copy your Support code (it looks like 3F9A-12C0).
  2. Email it to support@juakenya.co.ke with your request.
  3. We’ll reply within 7 days and, for a deletion, remove your anonymous player number and everything linked to it (results, reports and suggestions). Deleting can’t be undone and your past points are removed from County Wars.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Children and young people

Jua Kenya is meant for players aged 13 and over and is designed to need no personal details. We don’t knowingly collect more than described above from anyone. If you’re a parent or guardian and want your child’s data deleted, use the steps above.

Keeping it safe

All information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database only lets each phone add its own results; only our small team can see the rest, through a private admin site with logins, roles, and a log of every change.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
