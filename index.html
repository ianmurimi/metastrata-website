Dira Sports

dira (Kiswahili): compass, vision

68%of Strong picks were correct
2,730Premier League matches tested, 2019–2026

Know how likely each result is, before kick-off.

Dira Sports gives every English Premier League match a clear Home, Draw and Away probability, worked out from years of results and shot data. Each pick is marked by how confident the model is, so you know which calls are strong and which are a toss-up.

  • Home / Draw / Away probabilities for every Premier League fixture.
  • Confidence tiers: Strong, Lean and Toss-up, so you can see how sure each pick is.
  • Updated every morning with the latest fixtures and results.
  • Dira Pro, KES 299 a month: pay with M-Pesa or card.

Tested fairly: every match from August 2019 to October 2026 was predicted using only games played before it. Overall, 52.7% of top picks were right; bookmakers’ closing odds managed 54.8% on the same matches.

How well do you know Kenya?

Five questions a day on our history, counties, people, sport, food and Kiswahili. Learn something new with every answer, and earn points for your county.

  • Daily 5: the same five questions for everyone in Kenya, every day. About two minutes.

03 · Money · Coming soon

Hazina

hazina (Kiswahili): treasure, treasury

Your M-Pesa and bank texts, turned into a budget.

Hazina is a private money tracker for iPhone and Android. It reads the payment texts you already get from M-Pesa and your bank, sorts them for you, and shows what came in, what went out and what’s left this month.

  • No typing: payments arrive from M-Pesa and bank texts. Add cash by hand.
  • Budgets, bills and savings: plan your payday month and see spending against it.
  • People you support: track what you spend on and send to family.
  • Loans, debts and trips: what you owe, Fuliza and card bills, and budgets for time away.

Free to start

Everything is free for your first 14 days. After that the core app stays free, and Hazina Plus unlocks everything with a single one-time purchase. No subscription.
